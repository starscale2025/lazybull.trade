PHASE 1 — THE DESIGN SYSTEM RESURRECTION  (Days 3–6)
"From fake italics to a typeface that feels the tape."

-- FIXING THE FAUX SLANT: THE WONK IS A VOLATILITY METRIC --
  TRUTH FIRST (the Executioner would check): Fraunces has NO slnt axis.
  The slant lives in its true italic FAMILY — which we never shipped — while
  we preload 120KB of SOFT/WONK axes we never once move. So:
  1) app/layout.tsx: style: ["normal", "italic"] on Fraunces.
     globals.css: `font-synthesis-style: none;` — faux slants can never
     regress. Every accent line on the site becomes the drawn letterform
     the license was paying for.
  2) THE LIVING AXIS. The dead SOFT/WONK weight becomes the market's pulse:
     the quote poll (already ticking every 15s) writes one CSS variable —
       --market-wonk: clamp(0, realizedVol / VOL_CEIL, 1)
     Display headings subscribe:
       font-variation-settings: "WONK" var(--market-wonk),
                                "SOFT" calc(var(--market-wonk) * 60);
     Calm tape → composed, upright serifs. Violent tape → the letterforms
     themselves go slightly feral. 200ms house-curve transition, quantized
     to 0.1 steps so it breathes rather than jitters; frozen under
     prefers-reduced-motion. The type IS an indicator. Nobody has this.

-- THE OLIVE PURGE: RADAR BY DEFAULT, STEALTH BY CHOICE --
  The 2.3:1 microtype was a mood board billed to beginners' retinas. Invert
  the deal:
  · TOKEN SURGERY: marketing --fg-faint #4a4a44 → #767670 (≥4.5:1 on
    #050505); pro-theme faint #64707d → #8494a4; text floor 11px (12px on
    coarse pointers); the 8px tier (22 uses) is executed without appeal.
  · TWO READING MODES, honestly framed:
      RADAR  (default) — AA everywhere. The site you can actually read.
      STEALTH (opt-in toggle, persisted) — the low-glow whisper terminal for
      operators who want the fiction, labeled plainly: "sub-AA by choice."
    Accessibility is the default; the vibe is the reward, never the toll.
  · ACCENT RATIONING: one accent hue per viewport on marketing pages.
    Cyan demoted to data-semantic (info/alerts), amber to warnings,
    plasma retired. The headline formula (white roman + colored italic ×15
    files) is broken on half the pages — alternates already in the DNA:
    the mono-stamp, the number-led head, the underline sweep.

-- THE CAPTAIN'S CONSOLE: ENDING FLOATING ANARCHY --
  Six confirmed occlusions (mic over RESET FUNDS; PLACE A BET over the
  consensus meter, the leaderboard, the LEGAL DISCLAIMER) all trace to two
  components hardcoding the same corner. Structural cure, one component:
  · components/Dock.tsx — a single fixed slot manager, bottom-right,
    env(safe-area-inset-*) aware. Pages REGISTER occupants (voice, bet,
    teacher) via context; the Dock renders ONE console chip that fans out
    into a vertical tray on tap/hover; body reserves var(--dock-clearance).
  · Collapsed, it reads as a tiny status console (mic state · bet armed) —
    on-brand chrome instead of SaaS-blob confetti. No element may ever
    render fixed outside the Dock. Lint rule enforces it.

-- THE FIDELITY LADDER: SPEED AS A VISIBLE VIRTUE --
  The terminal re-renders 1,640 lines of SVG per raw mousemove. The sprint
  cure is imperative, not a canvas rewrite (that's the post-sprint ticket):
  · Crosshair + hover legend move OUT of React state into two ref'd nodes
    written directly inside ONE rAF-coalesced pointer handler. Pan coalesces
    setVp to rAF.
  · During high-velocity drags the chart sheds sub-pixel decoration (grid,
    legend values update at 10Hz) and restores on settle — with the house
    fast-attack/long-settle curve, so even the degrade is choreographed.
  · The status bar earns a tiny "60fps" tick. A terminal that ADVERTISES
    its frame budget is a flex no template can fake.

-- THE FILL RITUAL: MONEY MOMENTS GET A HEARTBEAT --
  Today an order fills and a number silently mutates. Three beats, one
  clock, ~600ms total, all springs from the existing motion stack:
  1) The working-order line flashes side-color and COLLAPSES into the fill
     point (the drag system already knows the geometry).
  2) A radial pulse bleeds outward from the fill price across the row —
     bull wash up, bear wash down. This moves because money moved.
  3) The P&L and account figures ROLL — odometer morph, decimal sliding
     into place (DecimatedNumber's mechanics, rebuilt ref-based per
     Phase 4, reused for a purpose instead of a Matrix cliché).
  Same ritual, smaller amplitude, for P&L tick direction. Reduced-motion:
  color change + toast only.

PHASE 3 — UX & THE INVISIBLE HAND  (Days 9–11)
"Make the keyboard a weapon, the phone a first-class desk, the narrator a voice."
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

-- THE OPERATOR'S DECK: A11Y AS A POWER-USER FEATURE --
  /pro is already keystroke-driven (type-to-search, Alt-tools). Finish the
  thought:
  · ⌘K COMMAND DECK — generalize the admin CommandPalette into a public
    layer: every action, fuzzy-searchable, each row showing its hotkey.
    First Tab press on /pro surfaces a one-time coach mark: "⌘K — the whole
    desk, no mouse."
  · The global :focus-visible ring renders as phosphor BRACKETS [ ] around
    the focused control — the focus system wearing the brand instead of
    fighting it. Tab order becomes a designed path, not an apology.

-- DRAG-TO-SHORT: KILLING THE RIGHT-CLICK PARADOX --
  Shorting via onContextMenu is impossible on iOS and invisible everywhere.
  · The chain goes Pointer Events end-to-end (pointerdown/pointermove hit-
    testing replaces the dead mouseover drag-select).
  · New grammar: tap = long. DRAG DOWNWARD past a threshold = short — the
    row tints bear as you pull it under, with a subtle notch haptic
    (navigator.vibrate where available). You literally pull the strike
    below the line. Intuitive, native to touch, and visually ours.
  · WCAG 2.5.1 honored: an explicit LONG/SHORT segmented control ships
    beside the gesture — gestures are never the only path.
  · ProbabilityCone handles: pointer events + setPointerCapture +
    touch-action:none, plus visible low/high number inputs. The /trade
    thesis band stops being frozen on half the web.

-- THE NARRATOR: SCREEN-READER POETRY, SHARED WITH THE VOICE --
  Instead of raw aria-live noise, one lib/narrator.ts composes market
  commentary from store events (the events pipeline already funnels them):
    "Filled — one hundred Apple at 326.90."
    "You're up twelve dollars. Stop holding two percent below."
    "Kill switch armed: book flattened."
  · Rendered to a polite aria-live region (fills assertive), throttled,
    deduplicated.
  · The SAME strings feed the voice co-pilot's captions — one narrative
    layer serving screen readers, voice, and toasts. The a11y layer stops
    being compliance and becomes the product's spoken voice.
  · The chart gains its words: a visually-hidden OHLC summary + arrow-key
    crosshair stepping. 1,255 bars finally have a sentence.

PHASE 4 — THE DURABILITY PATCH  (Days 11–12)
"Stop the clocks that don't tick. Free the memory that doesn't move."

-- ONE AMBIENT CLOCK TO RULE THE DECORATIONS --
  lib/ambient-clock.ts: a single shared ticker with IO-gated subscribers.
  · HungCard's FOUR immortal pendulum rAFs → four callbacks on one clock,
    asleep offscreen, at rest when settled.
  · ScrollScrub's per-frame getBoundingClientRect loop → starts/stops with
    its existing IntersectionObserver.
  · DecimatedNumber → writes textContent via ref inside the shared tick
    (no setState-per-frame; the quant run-all cascade stops stacking dozens
    of React commits).
  · document.hidden freezes PHYSICS while data polls continue — return to a
    snapshot, never a jittery replay.
  · GsapScroller: debounce the resize refresh; skip refresh when wire()
    attached nothing.
  · lazyLoop keeps observing: webm loops pause() on exit, play() on entry.
    The battery tax ends.

  seed tapes), WHAT'S YOURS (the paper account, the history). The
  Executioner called honesty our best asset; this makes it furniture.

-- THE LESSON THAT CONFESSES: "ANATOMY OF A BROKEN VWAP" --
  Scoped truthfully: the full choose-your-own-adventure /learn rebuild is
  a NEXT-sprint epic. What ships in THIS sprint is its flagship chapter —
  an interactive autopsy of our own Phase-0 bug:
  · A live chart cell with a toggle: BROKEN (the string-length session
    reset — the line you're seeing is just typical price) vs FIXED (true
    session-anchored VWAP snapping into place).
  · The teacher note tells the truth: "We shipped this bug. Here's how you
    catch it, and here's the test that now makes it impossible."
  · Ends by opening the VERIFIED MATH ✓ on /pro.
  A trading school that dissects its own defect in public is unforgettable
  in a way no phosphor glow will ever be. The bug becomes the syllabus.
